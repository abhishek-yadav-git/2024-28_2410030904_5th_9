## 🏢 About the Internship

The Data Analytics Job Simulation Internship at Interns of Industry focused on applying analytical thinking to practical business problems.

The internship emphasized understanding data, identifying meaningful patterns, developing risk profiles, exploring AI-based prediction, communicating insights through business reports, and using analytics to support collections strategy.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The major objectives of the internship were:

- To understand the practical data analytics workflow.
- To perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- To understand risk profiling using business data.
- To explore AI-based delinquency prediction.
- To develop business reporting and data storytelling skills.
- To understand AI-driven collections strategy.
- To improve analytical and problem-solving abilities.

---

## 📌 Projects / Tasks Completed

### 1. Exploratory Data Analysis and Risk Profiling

Performed analysis to understand the structure and characteristics of business data.

Key activities included:

- Data exploration
- Data quality checking
- Identifying patterns and trends
- Understanding relevant risk indicators
- Risk profiling and interpretation

### 2. Predicting Delinquency with AI

Explored an AI / machine-learning approach for predicting delinquency.

The workflow involved:

- Data preparation
- Feature understanding
- Model development
- Prediction
- Model evaluation

### 3. Business Report and Data Storytelling

Focused on converting analytical findings into meaningful business insights.

This included:

- Summarizing analytical findings
- Creating meaningful visualizations
- Identifying important patterns
- Communicating insights clearly
- Connecting data findings with business decisions

### 4. AI-driven Collections Strategy

Explored how data and AI-based insights can support collection activities.

The strategy involved:

- Identifying higher-risk cases
- Prioritizing cases
- Supporting collection decisions
- Monitoring outcomes
- Improving decisions using data
